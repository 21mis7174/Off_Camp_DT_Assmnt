================================================
PART A: FIND 25 TARGET COMPANIES
================================================

1. SELECT YOUR RESEARCH SCOPE:
   - City: Choose ONE from (Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Chennai, Coimbatore, 
     Vadodara, Bengaluru, Indore, Surat, Ludhiana, Kolhapur)
   - Segments: Choose 1-2 from:
     * Basket A (Specialty Manufacturing): Biotech, diagnostics, specialty chemicals, performance chemicals
     * Basket B (Adjacent Specialty): Complex APIs, medical devices, hybrid seeds, sensors, animal health
     * Basket C (Operator-Led): Technical textiles, precision auto, specialty food processing, artisanal manufacturing

2. THE "FEDERER COMPANY" PROFILE:
   Revenue: Rs. 50Cr - Rs. 500Cr
   Founder: Growth mindset + wants to build in-house capability (not outsource)
   Product: Differentiated, technical products/specialized services (not commodities)
   Decision-maker: Demonstrates systems thinking (founder with tech pedigree OR operator 
                   who built ERP/costing models/formal planning infrastructure)

3. ELIGIBILITY GATES (Must PASS both):
   E1 - Producer: Makes products/delivers specialized services in-house 
        (has own facility, plant, studio). NOT a trader/distributor/CRO/service lab.
   E2 - Accessible: Operational presence in India (HQ or primary operations).
        DT's Fellows work on-site.

4. SCORING CRITERIA - Federer Score (out of 100):
   C3 (20 pts): Differentiation - Patents, IP, regulatory approvals, specialized equipment, 
                proprietary processes
   C4 (15 pts): Decision-Maker Quality - PhD/IIT/scientist OR demonstrated systems thinking
   C5 (15 pts): Growing Sector - PLI eligible, China+1, Make-in-India, export tailwinds
   C6 (15 pts): Growth Signals - Need ≥2 of: hiring, facility expansion, new certifications, 
                active website, revenue growth
   C7 (20 pts): Systems Maturity - ERP/SAP implementation, structured costing, digital 
                infrastructure
   C8 (15 pts): Leadership Succession - Gen-2 on board with operational role, professional 
                managers from outside, distinct leadership portfolios

5. SCORING BANDS:
   80-100 (A): Strong Federer - High priority target
   60-79  (B): Probable Federer - Good target
   40-59  (C): Borderline - Research further, may have hidden disqualifier
   <40    (D): Not ICP - Do not include

6. AUTO-DISQUALIFY IF:
   - Trader, distributor, or importer
   - Service company (CROs, testing labs, clinical trials) unless proprietary specialized service
   - Generic pharma (bulk APIs, branded generics)
   - Subsidiary of large group or recently acquired
   - PE/VC-controlled (majority institutional ownership)
   - Revenue >Rs. 500Cr
   - No website or placeholder site
   - No visible activity in last 2 years

7. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY:
   - Use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot) as thinking partners, NOT for copy-paste output
   - Expected yield: ~30% (need to research 75-100 companies to find 25)
   - Verify all claims with actual evidence (LinkedIn, websites, MCA filings, Tofler, press releases)
   - For each company, provide: name, website, city, segment, what they make, revenue estimate,
     decision-maker (name/background), E1 & E2 gates, C3-C8 scores with evidence, overall score,
     verdict, and personalization hook (specific, true, recent detail for outreach)

PART A DELIVERABLES:
1. CSV/Spreadsheet with 25 companies (scored, with evidence for each criterion)
2. Methodology Document - How you found companies, sources used, research process, learnings
3. Code/Scripts (if used) - Scraping, API calls, AI prompts, data processing notebooks
4. Upload to GitHub or Google Drive, share link on platform
